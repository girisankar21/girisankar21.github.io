Fluid Dynamics & Aerosol Instrumentation Technology

Atmose · University of Waterloo · Mech & Mechatronics Engineering · Sep 2025 – Present

Conducting graduate research and industry R&D at Atmose (Edmonton, AB) focused on the physics of charged aerosol transport and precision measurement instrumentation. Work combines high-fidelity multiphysics simulation in OpenFOAM with experimental validation to improve next-generation aerosol measurement devices used in air quality and atmospheric science.

  • Developed a coupled multiphysics CFD model in OpenFOAM integrating charged aerosol transport and electrostatics to optimise Faraday cup electrometer geometry for minimal current leakage and maximum signal.
  • Investigated residence time distributions (RTD) in a radioactive neutralizer using CFD and experimental methods; redesigned internal flow geometry to reduce recirculation and improve neutralization performance.
  • First-authored two peer-reviewed manuscripts; co-authored a third — all under review (see Publications below).
